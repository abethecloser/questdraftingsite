Accessory dwelling units — ADUs, casitas, guest houses, call them what you like — are one of the most common projects we get asked about in Gilbert and across the East Valley. They add space for family, room for guests, or flexibility for the future, all on land you already own. But "can I build a casita?" almost always comes down to one thing: **your specific lot.**

## Start with the lot, not the floor plan

It is tempting to start by sketching the casita you want. In practice, the smarter first move is to understand what your parcel allows. The key factors are usually:

- **Setbacks** — how close to each property line you can build.
- **Lot coverage** — how much of your land can be under roof.
- **Size limits** — many jurisdictions cap accessory-unit square footage relative to the main home.
- **Utilities and access** — where water, sewer, and power can reach.

Design that respects these from day one avoids the heartbreak of falling in love with a plan the city will not approve.

## Attached, detached, or converted

Broadly, accessory space comes in three flavors:

1. **Detached casita** — a separate structure in the yard. The most flexible, and often the most desirable.
2. **Attached unit** — sharing a wall with the main home, sometimes simpler to service.
3. **Conversion** — turning existing space (a garage, a bonus room) into a livable unit.

Each has different implications for cost, privacy, and how the jurisdiction reviews it.

## Where the rules come from

Requirements vary by city and by zoning district, and they change over time. Rather than rely on what a neighbor did three years ago, it is worth confirming the **current** standards for your address before committing to a design. That is part of the up-front work we do so a project starts on solid ground.

## Design it once, permit it once

The homes that go smoothly are the ones designed with the rules and the site in mind from the first sketch, modeled in 3D so you can approve the real thing, then documented into a coordinated permit set. Done in that order, an ADU is one of the highest-value additions you can make to a property.
